Privacy Policy

Uni Notes
Last Updated: September 2026

Uni Notes ("we", "us", or "our") is a college study and notes management application designed to help students access and organize academic resources.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name and email address when you create or use an account.
  • User ID and authentication information.
  • Notes, PYQs, PDFs, ratings, reviews, and discussion content that you choose to submit.
  • Device and app information that may be collected by third-party services used by the app.
  • Advertising-related information when advertisements are displayed.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide authentication and account functionality.
  • To provide notes, PYQs, syllabus, and other study resources.
  • To store and display user-submitted academic content.
  • To manage ratings, reviews, and group discussions.
  • To send notifications where notification features are enabled.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve Uni Notes.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication: may be used for account authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore: may be used to store application data.
  • Cloudflare R2: may be used to store uploaded academic files.
  • Google AdMob: may be used to display advertisements.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

4. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the app.
  • User-submitted academic content may be visible to other users when the relevant feature is designed for sharing.
  • Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Protection

  • We use reasonable measures to protect information handled by Uni Notes.
  • No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
  • Do not upload confidential or sensitive information that you do not want to share.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You can contact us to request deletion of your account and associated personal information.

Some information may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate operational purposes.

7. User-Submitted Content

If you upload notes, PYQs, PDFs, ratings, reviews, or discussion content, that content may be processed and displayed according to the relevant Uni Notes feature. Do not upload private, confidential, or sensitive personal information that you are not authorized to share.

8. Notifications

Uni Notes may use notification services to provide relevant application or academic-resource notifications when notification features are enabled.

9. Children's Privacy

Uni Notes is intended for students and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, features, or data practices change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.
