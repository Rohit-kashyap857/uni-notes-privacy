Uni Notes – Account Deletion

Uni Notes allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Uni Notes account, send an email to:

uninotesapp8522142@gmail.com

Please use the subject: Account Deletion Request

Include the email address associated with your Uni Notes account so that we can identify the account and process your request.

Important: Account deletion may permanently remove your account and associated personal data. Some information may be retained when required by law or necessary for security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

What May Be Deleted

  • Account information
  • Authentication-related account data
  • Personal information associated with the account
  • User-associated application data where applicable

Processing Requests

We will review account deletion requests and take appropriate action to delete eligible account data.
